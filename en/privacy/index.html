Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

Thank you for using Vellum. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use the Vellum application ("Vellum").

Quick Summary

  • Vellum has no servers.
  • All your capsules are stored in your Apple iCloud account, encrypted end to end.
  • We never have access to your audio recordings, video recordings, or personal notes.
  • When you send a gift, the capsule travels directly through iCloud to the recipient. There is no intermediary between us.
  • We may collect anonymous usage statistics; these statistics do not personally identify you.

1. Who We Are

Vellum is developed by Vellum. For contact: vellumcapsuleapp@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data We Do Not Collect

Vellum does not collect, see, or store on our servers the following data:

  • Your audio recordings
  • Your video recordings
  • The content of your capsules
  • Your personal notes
  • Recipient information (phone numbers, names)
  • Metadata of capsules you've sent or received

All of this is stored in your Apple iCloud account. Vellum is just an interface — we have no servers of our own to access your data.

2.2 Data Managed by Apple

The following are managed via Apple iCloud services:

  • CloudKit data storage
  • Push notification delivery (Apple Push Notification service)
  • iCloud sync and backup

The privacy of this data is governed by Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

2.3 Anonymous Usage Data (Optional)

To improve Vellum, we may collect the following anonymous data:

  • How often the app is opened
  • Which features are used (e.g., "create capsule" tapped)
  • Crash reports and error statistics
  • App version, device model, iOS version

This data does not personally identify you. The content of your capsules is never included in these statistics.

If you don't want this data collected, you can disable it in iOS settings: - Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share with App Developers (turn off)

3. Third Parties

Vellum uses the following third-party services:

Service Purpose Data accessed
Apple iCloud / CloudKit Data storage Your encrypted capsules
Apple Push Notification Notification delivery Empty notification payloads
RevenueCat Purchase transactions Anonymous transaction ID

Vellum does not sell or share any data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Gift Feature and Data Sharing

When you send a capsule as a gift to someone:

  1. A CKShare is created in your iCloud account (Apple's native sharing system).
  2. A link (Universal Link) is sent to the recipient.
  3. When the recipient opens the link, the capsule is copied to their iCloud account.
  4. The capsule is now in the recipient's account.

Throughout this process, Vellum stores no data on its servers. The entire transfer happens through Apple iCloud.

Recipient information such as phone number and name is kept in the capsule's metadata and stays in your iCloud account until the capsule reaches the recipient. When they accept, the metadata transfers to them.

5. Data Retention

Vellum capsules are kept as long as you keep them or until you close your account. There is no automatic deletion. Your capsules are yours.

Anonymous usage statistics are retained by Apple Analytics for 7-14 days.

6. Your Data Rights

As an Apple iCloud user, you have the following rights (through Apple):

  • Right to access: You can view your capsules through iCloud Drive.
  • Right to delete: You can delete them anytime from the app or iCloud settings.
  • Right to portability: Apple lets you export your iCloud data (privacy.apple.com).
  • Right to rectification: Your capsules are yours, you can change them as you wish.

For data requests specifically about Vellum: vellumcapsuleapp@gmail.com

7. Children's Privacy

Vellum is not directed at children under 13. If we become aware that a user is under 13, we will contact Apple to have the account deleted.

8. International Data Transfers

Apple stores iCloud data in its global data centers. Details are in Apple's privacy policy.

9. Policy Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will send an in-app notification when there is a significant change.
