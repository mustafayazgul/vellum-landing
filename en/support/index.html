Frequently Asked Questions

How does Vellum work?

Vellum lets you create audio and video capsules and open them at a future date. You create a capsule, record it, choose the opening date (1 month, 1 year, up to 25 years). Until that date arrives, the capsule stays closed — even you cannot listen to it.

Where are my capsules stored?

In your Apple iCloud account. Vellum has no servers. All capsules are stored in iCloud, encrypted end to end. We have no access to capsule content.

Will my capsules be lost if I delete Vellum?

No. Your capsules remain in iCloud. When you reinstall Vellum and sign in with the same Apple ID, your capsules come back.

What if I change my iCloud account?

Capsules stay with your old account. If you sign in with a new account, your capsules won't appear. Check your capsules before any Apple ID changes.

Can I open a capsule before its time?

No. This is Vellum's core principle. Until the date arrives, no one — including you — can listen to the capsule. This is an intentional design decision.

How do I send a capsule as a gift?

  1. Create a new capsule.
  2. Select "Send to someone."
  3. Enter the recipient's phone number (must be linked to their Apple ID).
  4. Choose an opening date.
  5. Record, purchase, send.

The recipient receives a link. When they tap it, the capsule is added to their iCloud account.

I sent a gift but the recipient didn't see it.

A few possibilities:

  • The recipient's phone number may not be linked to their Apple ID. Ask them to check.
  • The link may not have been delivered yet; make sure you sent it via iMessage, email, etc.
  • The recipient's iCloud connection may be off.

I'm out of free capsules, now what?

You'll need to make an in-app purchase for new capsules. Single capsule and bundle options are available. There are no subscriptions.

I want a refund for a purchased capsule.

Apple App Store refund policy applies. You can request via https://reportaproblem.apple.com

What devices does Vellum work on?

iOS 17 and above. Optimized for iPhone and iPad. Mac, Apple Watch, and Apple TV are not supported (yet).

Can I suggest a feature?

Yes, please write to feedback@vellumapp.net. We read every suggestion.

What happens to my capsules if Vellum shuts down?

Your capsules continue to live in Apple iCloud. Since Vellum has no servers, even if the company closes, you won't lose data. You can access the raw files through Apple iCloud Drive.
